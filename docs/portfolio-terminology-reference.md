## Writing direction to preserve

- Connect visual storytelling, film and television VFX, commercials, Houdini, AI filmmaking, immersive and interactive experiences, and creative tools as one creative practice.
- Show creative judgment and the technical ability to carry an idea through production.
- Keep the introduction natural, thoughtful, confident, curious, and approachable.
- Briefly include the singer/dancer background and two and a half years performing in a Broadway show. Keep the emphasis on current work; do not invent the show's name or force a connection to every project.
- Avoid corporate language, inflated claims, generic innovation language, and long software lists. Do not imply that personal experiments were client commissions.
- Explain each project's idea, purpose, personal contribution, and current state. Flag missing facts instead of inventing them.

## Terminology reference

These are names for ideas Gordo described, not vocabulary to force into every paragraph. The descriptions below structure and condense the pasted discussion.

### Story and creative tools

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Narrative structure | Arrange events so a story builds and makes sense; connecting images into a story is harder than producing an isolated image. |
| Branching storylines | Alternative paths through a story, chosen by a person or suggested by a system. |
| Creative continuity | Keep imagery, dialogue, and ideas consistent and connected. |
| Structured story beats | Define moments of story progression that production or agents can work around. |
| LLM orchestration | Coordinate tools and generation steps around the story using a language model. |

### Music-video systems

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Music analysis | Extract structure and timing, such as verses, choruses, bridges, onsets, and musical inflections. |
| Semantic understanding | Use meaning and context, including lyrics, alongside timing. Verify what the system actually analyzes before claiming this. |
| Visual analysis | Detect scenes, divide footage into useful segments, and describe what happens in them. |
| Local microservices architecture | Specialized services on the computer/home server work together. Use only where the actual architecture supports the term. |
| Trade-offs | Compare the gains, costs, and constraints of approaches such as local processing and hosted APIs. |

### Continuity and evaluation

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Technical continuity | Shots fit together without unintended inconsistencies. |
| Character continuity | The more specific question of whether a character remains consistent across shots. The source used technical continuity as the umbrella term. |
| Narrative coherence | The sequence communicates a connected story; each shot carries the story into the next. |
| LLM as a judge | A model evaluates an output against defined requirements. |
| Multi-model evaluation | Compare assessments from multiple models. |
| Disagreement analysis | Investigate why evaluations differ; agreement alone does not establish correctness. |
| Human-in-the-loop validation | Keep human judgment and final creative approval in the review process. |

### Why the tools matter

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Creative iteration | Explore and refine successive versions. |
| Production efficiency | Reduce unnecessary work in making the result. |
| Time-cost-quality trade-offs / production triangle | Balance schedule, budget, and quality. |
| Creative flexibility | Create more opportunities to try cuts or directions. “More creative at-bats” is a metaphor, not a technical term. |
| Technical friction | Obstacles and repetitive tasks that interrupt creative work. |

### Musicality and editing

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Continuity | Connected flow between shots, including motion and camera movement. |
| Invisible editing | Cuts do not pull attention away from the experience. |
| Rhythmic synchronization / beat-aligned editing / beat synchronization | Related descriptions of timing edits to musical rhythm. Choose the clearest wording for the specific feature. |
| Seamless continuity | A transition feels uninterrupted. Treat this as an intended creative result, not a guarantee. |
| Editing as choreography / visual rhythm | Shape the edit around beats, vocal phrasing, and movement, allowing different elements to lead. |
| Multimodal alignment | Connect sound, language, and imagery meaningfully. The choreography explanation may be clearer and more memorable in portfolio prose. |

### Improving the system

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Iterative optimization | Improve a working system through repeated passes. |
| Performance profiling | Measure where time and resources are spent. Use when measurements were actually taken. |
| Bottleneck analysis | Identify what limits overall performance. |

### Interview practice

| Term | Plain meaning / idea from the discussion |
| --- | --- |
| Retrieval | Bring the intended word or explanation to mind while speaking. |
| Guided rehearsal | Answer first, hear a clearer formulation, and try again in one's own words. |

## Example phrasing from the discussion

These are discussion examples to consider together, not approved website copy or verified capability claims:

> One problem I ran into making AI films is that it’s easy to get an image, hard to get a coherent story.

> I built agents around structured story beats and used an LLM to orchestrate generation across those beats.

> I built an automated music video editing system that uses musical structure and lyrical meaning to drive cuts.

> I use beat synchronization, but the goal is seamless continuity so the story feels uncut.

> I built a tool that strips away the technical grind of music video editing. It analyzes music and footage so editors spend less time assembling and more time telling the story.

Before using these publicly, verify the specific project's implementation, the extent of automation, lyrical analysis, evaluation behavior, and Gordo's contribution. Borrow their clarity without adopting claims that the evidence does not support.
